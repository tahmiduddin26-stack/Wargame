## Base description

Open the battle notebook. Rally a scrappy army, defend your fort and turn a sketched battlefield into a glorious mess.

Doodlebook Battles is a landscape lane strategy game about choosing when to spend, hold the line and evolve. Begin with clubs and slings, then advance through medieval, gunpowder, modern and future armies.

- Take on 16 campaign operations with four difficulty settings.
- Command 20 units, build emplacements and unleash era specials.
- Hold out against endless waves in The Long Watch.
- Earn commander XP and credits for skills across three branches.
- Equip earned army paints and track your service record.
- Play campaign and survival offline, with save backup and restore.

One lane. Two forts. Plenty of trouble.
